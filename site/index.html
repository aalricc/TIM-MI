When your app breaks, MI fixes it.

MI is a small service you run next to your app. It checks your app every second. If something breaks, it finds the cause, fixes it, and makes sure your data came back too. No one gets paged.

How you use it

MI isn’t a plugin or a library. It’s one container that sits beside your app and talks to it over the network.

  1. Add MI next to your appOne extra service in your Docker Compose file.
  2. Point it at your appGive it the address of your app’s health check. Your app also shares its logs and backups.
  3. Leave it runningOpen the dashboard to see every check, every fix and why it was made.
# docker-compose.yml
services:
  your-app:
    image: your-app:latest

  mi:
    image: ghcr.io/aalricc/tim-mi-agent
    environment:
      # where your app's health check lives
      MI_TARGET_SERVICE_URL: http://your-app:8080
      MI_MONITOR_HEALTH_PATH: /health/deep
    env_file: .env   # your AI keys
    ports:
      - "8090:8090"  # MI's dashboard

What happens when something breaks

The same four steps, every time. If a fix doesn’t work, MI tries a different one until it does.

It notices

The health check fails. MI opens an incident within a second and collects what your app is reporting.

It finds the cause

MI’s AI reads the evidence and says what is wrong, in plain words, with the proof it used.

It makes one fix

Several fixes are proposed. A separate checker picks one, and safety rules make sure it’s allowed.

It checks the result

Your app has to pass its health check and have its data back. If not, MI keeps going.

It gets better every time

Each fix is scored by how much of your data it kept. MI uses those scores to choose the right fix sooner next time.

Built to be trusted with your app

Only safe actions

MI can do four things: restore a backup, run a database fix, replace a file inside your app’s folder, or restart. Anything else is refused.

One step at a time

Exactly one fix per try, and every fix is checked by a separate health test before MI moves on.

Everything on record

Every check, cause, choice and result is saved and shown on the dashboard, so you can see why MI did what it did.

Under the hood
  • Two AI calls per new problem: one finds the cause, one proposes fixes. The prompts describe the evidence and the allowed actions only, with no hints about which fix to use.
  • A separate decision model, JEV, scores the proposed fixes and must approve the one that runs.
  • Relevant database documentation is looked up for each problem and given to the AI.
  • Fixes that worked are remembered per type of problem and reused once they have proven themselves.
  • Today MI works with apps backed by SQLite that share a health check, logs, database status and backups over HTTP.
